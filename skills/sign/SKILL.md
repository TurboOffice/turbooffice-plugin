name: sign
description: "In TurboOffice, a document to send out for signature that is not one of the deal's instruments — \"get the attorney's signature on this exhibit\", \"prep this affidavit to sign\""
---

A document that needs signatures and has no place in the deal's record of instruments — an exhibit, an affidavit, a brokerage's own form. It needs the PDF: `work_on({ files })` reads the files you name, `{ artifactId }` from `## Files in this conversation` or `{ documentId }` from the deal's documents on file. A photo of a page is an image, not a PDF, and the tool says so; with no PDF, ask for it rather than starting an empty envelope. It reads the named PDFs (splits a packet into its documents, matches each to a form edition when there is one), builds the signing envelope with a tab for every signer role the form carries, less any line the paper already carries ink on, and links it in the chat — the editor behind that link is where the user reviews the tabs and sends. Each call is one envelope, so paper for several envelopes is several calls; `envelopeId` puts more paper on a draft already started. The receipt names the roles that still need a signer: end the reply by asking for each third party's name and email — you add them with `act_on_envelope`'s `change_signers`, the user does not — and by offering to set the people the receipt says are already on the deal — do not send the user to the editor for that. When the user says to send it, `act_on_envelope({ envelopeId, action: { intent: "review_and_send" } })` puts its send in front of them with what still stops it; the send itself is theirs, on the paper. Pass `dealId` when the paper belongs to a deal on your roster (a chosen deal is used without asking); with no deal it starts on its own. Nothing is drafted: the paper is what it is. Paper that is one of the deal's instruments — a representation agreement, an offer, a counter, an amendment, a termination — is route-document whatever its signatures: the router reads what it is, prepares its signing on the deal's own paper (the agreement the deal owes, the offer it records) or proposes the deal it starts, where a standalone envelope would be signed without the deal ever learning of it.

Its tools here: act_on_envelope. The rest of its work runs in Turbo Office's own assistant, not here: hand it to `work_on` with the person's words and what you know (`request`; `dealId` when you know the deal; `threadId` to say more on a thread it started). Where this guide names `work_on` with arguments, put them in the request's words.
