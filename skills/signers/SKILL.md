---
name: signers
description: "In TurboOffice, who signs and where: add, replace or remove a signer on a draft or on paper out for signatures, add a seller or buyer to a deal, take one off or put one on or off title (a listing or buyer agreement's signers follow the deal), add, move or remove a draft's signing fields, put them back to the form's own — \"add a signer to the listing agreement\", \"Jordan's signature is on the wrong line\", \"reset the form\""
---

`act_on_envelope({ envelopeId, action: { intent: "change_signers", change } })` adds, replaces or removes a signer on any envelope of the deal, at any stage — `envelopeId` is the id printed as envelope id (the chain receipt, `list_files`): a draft changes at once, and paper out for signatures gets an approval card. `change_parties({ change })` adds a seller or buyer to the chosen deal (`use_deal`), takes one off it, puts one on or off title, or changes one's name, email or phone, as an approval card — the door for a person who belongs on the listing or buyer agreement, whose seats follow the deal's sellers and buyers. `act_on_envelope({ envelopeId, action: { intent: "reset_layout" } })` takes every added field off a draft and puts the form's own fields back for the seated roles. A person who belongs on the listing or buyer agreement is added to the deal, never seated on the draft: its seats are the deal's sellers or buyers. `act_on_envelope({ envelopeId, action: { intent: "change_fields", changes } })` adds, moves, removes or re-assigns a draft's signing fields, every change of a request in one call; paper that matched no form arrives with none, and you place them this way. Read the document first (`read_document`): it shows the blanks, the fields already there and any ink, with the ids a change names, and a page's picture and boxes when you name one. What is refused, and the door that works: a seat that already signed is never re-seated in place (the paper is pulled back and prepared again); a listing or buyer agreement draft's seats follow the deal's roster (`change_parties` changes the person on the deal and the draft reseats itself); and a seat held by a person on the roster. The form's roles are the seats by default, and any role the catalog knows seats a person the form has no line for, named by key (buyer 2, WITNESS, ADDITIONAL_PARTY_1) or family (seller, buyer): the paper's own free seat when it has one, else a seat with no line yet, which the receipt says; a line placed for that role with `change_fields` gives the seat its line, in either order, and a seat still without a line when the draft is sent is left off. `act_on_envelope({ envelopeId, action: { intent: "move", dealId } })` puts a signing envelope on another deal of yours, or with null off its deal (staff), at once, its seats re-pinned to that deal's roster: the door for paper started on the wrong deal or on none. It is refused, naming the door, for paper a deal's record stands on: an offer draft on a deal (withdraw it and start it there), an offer out for signatures or signed (route_document files its paper on the deal and records the offer there), an envelope an offer or agreement is recorded from, and typed paper (agreements, disclosures, terminations, amendments), which is voided and prepared again on the right deal. `reset_layout` on paper out for signatures (its fields belong to the ceremony; the way back is to void it), while a client is filling it, or on paper with no form to go back to. `change_parties` refuses a person already on the deal (who signs an envelope is `change_signers`), a buyer on a listing or a seller on a buy deal, a roster change on a closed deal, and an email the client already signs in or signed with (support changes it, and the refusal says where the user reaches support). An added field may overlap another field or a mark on the page; it still lands and the receipt says how much, and whether a line is already signed is yours to read.

Its tools: act_on_envelope, read_document, change_parties.
