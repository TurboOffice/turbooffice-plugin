name: deal
description: "In TurboOffice, the deal itself or its clients: archive a deal or take it out of the archive, move it between the stages before a contract, change a listing's list price, close a deal (one whose contract was signed outside the app too) or correct a closed deal's price, day or commission, move a contract's closing date, share uploaded files with the clients, correct a seller's or buyer's name, email or phone — \"archive the old Kaden deal\", \"send the inspection to the sellers for their records\", \"Kaden's email is wrong, it's kaden@gmail.com\", \"we're live, mark it listed\", \"drop the list price to 425\", \"it closed Oct 7 at 262k\", and a sale that already closed off the platform, recorded so it counts in the agent's closed numbers — \"add my closing on 4 Elm St from March, 412k, 3%\""
---

`act_on_deal({ action })` acts on the chosen deal: `unarchive`, `stage`, `list_price`, `go_live`, and `archive` on a deal with nothing out for signatures, run at once and its receipt says what changed; everything else, archiving paper out for signatures included, is an approval card. `archive` with a `reason` (`CLIENT_NOT_TRANSACTING`, `LOST_TO_OTHER_AGENT`, `LISTING_EXPIRED` for a listing, `OTHER`), `unarchive` (back to the stage it was archived in), `stage` (`LISTING_PRE_LIST` or `LISTING_LISTED` on a listing, `BUYER_SETUP` or `BUYER_TOURING` on a buy deal), `list_price` with `price` in dollars on a listing, `share_documents` with the `documentIds` of files uploaded to the deal (the ids `list_files` prints as document id; a file sent in the chat is filed on the deal first), which emails the deal's clients that the files are on their deal page, `send_contract_copy`, which emails the signed copy filed on a contract recorded as signed outside the app (filing that copy emails nobody) to the people seated on that paper here, the deal's clients and the deal team, as the card names them, `close` with `closingDate` and `salePrice` on any deal that is not archived or paused (one with no contract recorded closes as a sale that went under contract outside the app), or on a closed deal to record its close again when the price, day or commission on record is wrong, `contract_date` with a contract date's `key` (date= in the brief: the closing, the due-diligence end, any date the contract states) and its new `date` on a deal under contract, recorded on its contract without an amendment: the door for a date the parties have moved without papering it. A date the record got wrong is a correction, and a move the parties still have to sign is an amendment; both are the contract skill's. `go_live` with the `date` a listing goes or went live: it sets the date, and the stage moves with `stage`. A deal's pricing plan, commission or fees and its listing or engagement period are `act_on_deal` `pricing`, which the agreement-terms skill explains. Archiving voids the deal's open paper and cancels its open tasks, and the receipt or card says which; unarchiving does not bring that paper back. A contract's stages come from its paper: under contract is the signed contract, and a termination ends it (the contract skill). A price the parties agree on an offer or contract is a change to its terms (the contract skill). The deal is the one the user names: find it among their deals by what they say (the address, the clients, "the old one", "the one with the wrong email"), choose it with `use_deal`, and the receipt or card names it. `change_parties({ change: { intent: "contact", person, name?, email?, phone? } })` corrects a seller's or buyer's details on every deal they are on, as an approval card; an email the client already signs in or signed with is refused: support changes it, and the refusal says where the user reaches support.

A record of the past: `record_closed_deal` creates a new deal already closed from what the user stated, and nothing is drafted or sent. A sale on a deal the person already has (their listing or buy deal at that address) is never recorded here: find that deal among theirs by the address, and close it with `act_on_deal` `close` above. `record_closed_deal` refuses such a sale and names the deal.

Its tools: act_on_deal, change_parties, record_closed_deal.
